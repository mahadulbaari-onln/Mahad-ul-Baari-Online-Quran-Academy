Structured Online Quran & Islamic Courses

Noorani Qaida & Noor Al Bayan

The ultimate foundation for kids and complete beginners to master Arabic letters, proper articulation points (Makharij), and short vowels.

Key Learning Outcomes:
  • Master Arabic alphabet with precise Makharij
  • Correct pronunciation of Harakat, Tanween & Sukoon
  • Connecting letters into words & full sentences
  • Smooth transition into direct Quranic reading

Quran Reading with Tajweed (Nazra)

Recite the Holy Quran fluently with classical Tajweed rules as recited by Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) under certified Ijazah scholars.

Key Learning Outcomes:
  • Fluent recitation without hesitation or errors
  • Rules of Noon & Meem Sakinah, Ghunnah & Ikhfa
  • Madd prolongation (2, 4, 6 counts) & Qalqalah
  • Ahkam al-Waqf (proper stopping and starting rules)

  • Regular tests & official Hifz certification
